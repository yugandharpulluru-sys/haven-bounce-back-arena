# Asset credits

The following resources are included in the game. Authors and source pages are recorded even where attribution is optional. All listed sources state CC0. This does not override your game jam's own rules; the team should check those separately.

## Sci-Fi Essentials Kit — Quaternius

Source: https://opengameart.org/content/sci-fi-essentials-kit and https://quaternius.itch.io/sci-fi-essentials-kit

Licence: CC0 1.0, as stated in the downloaded Standard pack's `Readme.txt` and the source page. The included models and textures are a selection from the free Standard pack: Eye Drone, Quad Shell, Trilobite, Rifle, Pistol, Sniper, and Barrel. Models are in `assets/quaternius/`.

## Sci-fi soldier — Irondust

Source: https://opengameart.org/content/sci-fi-soldier

Licence: CC0 1.0, as stated on the source page. The three soldier appearances in `assets/characters/` were exported from the original Blender file, with separate head/skin selections and resized textures. These are used for the visible opponents in private online matches. Basic leg movement is generated in code; these files do not contain authored combat animations.

## Sci-Fi Sounds — Kenney

Source: https://opengameart.org/content/sci-fi-sounds

Licence: CC0 1.0, as stated in the downloaded archive. Selected `.ogg` files are in `assets/audio/`.

## Concrete Floor 02 and Industrial Sunset Pure Sky — Poly Haven

Sources: https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_02 and https://polyhaven.com/a/industrial_sunset_puresky

Licence: CC0 1.0 under https://polyhaven.com/license. The floor colour and roughness maps and the 1K HDR sky are in `assets/polyhaven/`.
